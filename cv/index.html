Selected research

Geometry-Grounded Synthetic Data for Visual Recognition

Parameterized 3D defect models generate rendered imagery, pixel-level masks, and physical defect attributes without manual annotation.

  • Developed a synthetic-data framework that derives supervision directly from controllable defect geometry.
  • Generated 72K synthetic crack images and evaluated transfer across three public real-image segmentation benchmarks; realism enhancement improved zero-shot IoU by 3.7–13.6 points.
  • Developing a CAD-referenced extension for multi-view appearance and geometry representations, matching, and few-shot asset recognition.

Vision-language models and datasets for generating and evaluating reports from visual observations.

  • Co-developed VisualSiteDiary, a detector-free vision-language Transformer for construction photolog captioning and image retrieval (equal contribution).
